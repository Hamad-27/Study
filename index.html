📝 Quick Notes

💧 Science — Water Cycle

The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between the Earth and atmosphere.


1. Evaporation

Heat changes water into water vapour.

2. Condensation

Water vapour cools and forms clouds.

3. Precipitation

Water falls from clouds as rain, snow or hail.
