🧠 Chapter Quiz

📝 Questions & Answers

Practice questions from different Social Science topics.

Q1. What are natural resources? Natural resources are materials and features obtained from nature that people use for their needs.
Q2. What is universal adult franchise? It means eligible adult citizens have the right to vote without discrimination based on social or economic status.
Q3. What are the main factors of production? Land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship are commonly identified as factors of production.
Q4. What is a judiciary? The judiciary is the system of courts that interprets laws and provides justice according to the Constitution and laws.
Q5. What is urbanisation? Urbanisation is the growth of towns and cities and the increasing share of people living in urban areas.

⚡ Quick Notes

Short points for fast study.

🌱 Resources

Natural resources come from nature. They may be renewable or non-renewable and should be used carefully.

🗳️ Elections

Elections allow citizens to choose representatives. Voting is an important part of democratic participation.

🏛️ Government

The legislature makes laws and the executive implements government decisions.

⚖️ Judiciary

Courts interpret laws, settle disputes and protect constitutional principles.

🔄 Quick Revision

Remember these important ideas.

  • Natural resources are obtained from nature.
  • Resources should be used sustainably.
  • India has a democratic electoral system.
  • Citizens participate in democracy through voting.
  • The Parliament is the national legislature.
  • The executive carries out government policies and laws.
  • Land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship are factors of production.
  • The judiciary is an important organ of government.
  • Population changes through births, deaths and migration.
  • Urbanisation is associated with the growth of towns and cities.
  • India has a rich and diverse architectural heritage.
  • India's freedom struggle involved many people and movements.
